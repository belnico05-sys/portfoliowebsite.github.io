04 — Projects and Awards

Drone Concept
from
Scratch

At the age of 19, for my high school graduation project, I challenged myself to design and build a quadcopter drone from scratch.

My objective was to gain hands-on experience with a full engineering lifecycle. I realized the 3D CAD model and printed the frame via a PLA filament printer. Thanks to my brother, who took care of the electronics and helped me with the code, we were able to design a custom joystick controller for manual piloting. Everything running on a finely coded Arduino Nano and a few other components necessary to make a PID algorithm effective, such as the classic MPU-6050. Ultimately, the prototype never flew due to motor synchronization issues and lack of resources.

Despite the unsatisfactory outcome the project gave me a fundamental skill set in C++, electronics, and manufacturing.

Fondazione
Talento all’Opera
Award

In May 2025, following the SP2ARK Seasonal School at the Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna, I was awarded the Premio al Merito by the Fondazione Il Talento all'Opera — a Pisa-based philanthropic foundation with the mission of promoting academic excellence and equal opportunity in higher education.

The prize is granted to at most three students per Seasonal School edition who achieve the highest score in the programme's final evaluation. SP2ARK — Space Plasma Propulsion: Advancements, Research and Know-how — brought together bachelor's, master's, and doctoral students from all the world for an intensive week of frontier-research lectures and interdisciplinary study regarding electric propulsion systems.
