Rugby

ASD Rugby Gubbio 1984 · 2016–2025 · Second row & Flanker

Playing competitive rugby for over a decade with Rugby Gubbio 1984, including an early promotion to the Senior First Team (Serie B) as an Under-18 player, served as my primary school of character. Dedicating myself to an intensive schedule of five weekly training sessions and Sunday matches cultivated elite levels of discipline, accountability, and mental resilience.

The sport instilled a profound "team-first" mentality, teaching me to absorb setbacks, correct errors immediately, and actively cover for teammates under pressure. Ultimately, though I made the difficult choice to pivot my focus toward academic pursuits during university, I carry these core values forward, being always ready to "keep carrying the ball".

Finance

Personal Finance & Investing · Since 2021

Since 2021, I have cultivated my interests in personal finance, transitioning from empirical learning via virtual brokers to managing real capital. Failures

Experiences and Life Lessons

Be aware, the following are just the main ones. Life is full of failures, and in my understanding, growing up also means being ready when they come, so that you can stand up and keep pushing towards your goals.
As a rugby player, I suffered several injuries, and every one of them was due to a lack of attention or technique. So every big stop in my sporting career reminded me of what I should have improved before getting on the field, whether it was stamina, diet or technique.
Then, of course, there was the failed attempt at launching a dropshipping e-commerce platform without the required resources, that was a big one.
But my biggest failure was probably trying to get into the Italian Air Force Academy and being sent home after just a few days in Pozzuoli. That profoundly changed my perspective and led me to where I am today. I remember arriving at the train station to head back home while filling out the application form for the BSc in Aerospace Engineering at the University of Pisa. Without that day, this website probably wouldn't exist.
And don't even get me started on the several bad grades I got during my years at university, and I'm still counting!
So, long story short, if I learned something is that failing is a great way to understand where to steer your focus and your energy, whether in sports, studies, or life in general. The really hard part isn't failing itself, but accepting that it happened and understanding how to keep it from happening again.
